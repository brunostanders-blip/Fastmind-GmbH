1️⃣ THE PRODUCT & THE MARKET

SKTEC High-Performance Macro Fibers — Core Feature

SKTEC are polymer fibers (UHMWPE) for concrete reinforcement with patented siloxane-based surface treatment. Core advantage: slip-hardening effect.
