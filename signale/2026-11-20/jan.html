Robotics from China, opportunities in DACH: 20 November 2026

Signals for Jan from the Chinese robotics and humanoid market.

China signals

1. 8i Robotics surgical robot in six hospitals including Europe; CE certification under way (relevance: medium)
  • Status: the surgical system "春风化雨 8i Robotics", incubated and funded by 三友医疗 Sanyou Medical (Shanghai, SSE 688085), is installed for research and clinical use in six hospitals in North America, Asia and Europe, according to the company.
  • Approval: the system is in a GCP-registered clinical trial; EU CE certification is being pursued.
  • Appearance: presented at the 18th congress of the Chinese Orthopaedic Association (COA).
  • Source: „三友医疗：公司孵化的8i Robotics手术机器人已在六家医院完成科研和临床装机“ (Sanyou Medical: its incubated 8i Robotics surgical robot is installed in six hospitals for research and clinical use), 19 Nov 2026, Securities Times via Sina. Type: trade media quoting a company spokesperson.
  • Conformity in the source: yes, the source names CE certification as in progress, without reference to the EU MDR.
  • Notified body and certificate number named: no.
  • Approval in DACH: before the CE certificate the system may be used in Germany and Austria only within clinical investigations. The known European installation is in France, at the university hospital Amiens-Picardie (OrthoWorld).
  • Sales: no sales partner in DACH is named.
  • Switzerland: after CE certification the maker needs a Swiss authorised representative and registration in swissdamed to sell to Swiss hospitals; Swissmedic supervises the market.
  • Opportunity for Fastmind: guide 8i Robotics through the EU MDR and win DACH hospitals for the clinical evaluation in spine surgery.
  • Contributes to the strategic question: yes.
2. Black Sesame sets up a robotics chip headquarters in Shenzhen (relevance: medium)
  • Headquarters: 黑芝麻智能 Black Sesame Technologies (HKEX 2533) set up an embodied AI headquarters in Qianhai (Shenzhen) on 19 Nov 2026 and opened a showroom.
  • Chips: the Huashan and Wudang chip series cover 0.5 to more than 1,000 TOPS per chip, according to the report.
  • Partners: strategic cooperation with Midea, COSCO Shipping and Joyson Electronics; plus mass-production orders from several leading robot makers, not named.
  • Use: wheeled robots, humanoids, quadrupeds, industrial arms, mobile platforms and robotic mowers.
  • Source: „黑芝麻智能深圳前海成立具身智能总部 覆盖0.5至1000TOPS算力图谱“ (Black Sesame sets up embodied AI headquarters in Qianhai, computing power from 0.5 to 1,000 TOPS), 19 Nov 2026, Guandian via Sina, also People's Daily wire via Sina. Type: trade media and news agency, not a company statement.
  • Conformity in the source: no, nothing on CE, functional safety or the Cyber Resilience Act.
  • Notified body and certificate number named: no.
  • Supply: robotics makers in DACH gain another source of computing chips from China that addresses robotics directly.
  • Risk: a robotics product with these chips needs evidence under the Cyber Resilience Act and the EU Machinery Regulation from 20 Jan 2027 for Europe (EUR-Lex); the source says nothing on this.
  • Switzerland: machines with CE marking are recognised in Switzerland.
  • Opportunity for Fastmind: introduce a vetted second chip source from Shenzhen to DACH robotics makers and clarify the European evidence in advance.
  • Contributes to the strategic question: no.
3. Chinese distributor shows joint drives and robot battery systems at electronica Munich (relevance: low)
  • Fair: 商络电子 Sunlord Electronics (SZSE 300975) showed motor servo, robot bus and joint drive solutions and a 48 V battery management system for robots at electronica 2026 in Munich.
  • Purpose: the solutions are based on products of the makers it represents and serve technical support and the introduction of these products; they are not a main revenue source.
  • Source: „商络电子：在德国慕尼黑电子展展示多类方案产品“ (Sunlord Electronics: several solutions shown at the Munich electronics fair), 19 Nov 2026, investor platform answer via Sina; fair dates 10 to 13 Nov 2026 (Computer&AUTOMATION). Type: primary source, company answer to investors.
  • Conformity in the source: no.
  • Notified body and certificate number named: no.
  • Supply: DACH makers of drives, sensors and battery systems reach Chinese robot makers through Chinese distributors; the company seeks this exchange at a German fair.
  • Switzerland: machines and components with CE marking are recognised in Switzerland.
  • Opportunity for Fastmind: connect DACH component makers with Chinese robotics distributors.
  • Contributes to the strategic question: yes.
