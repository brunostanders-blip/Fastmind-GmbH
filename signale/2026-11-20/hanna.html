Regulation and compliance update

  • Machinery Regulation: applies from 20.01.2027; machines placed on the market until 19.01.2027 may still follow the Machinery Directive (Robanchor).
  • Standards list: the implementing decision with the first list of harmonised standards under the regulation is being prepared by the Commission and is to take over most existing standards (ibf).
  • Robot standards: EN ISO 10218-1 and -2:2025 give presumption of conformity under the Machinery Directive since Decision (EU) 2026/2015 (EUR-Lex).
  • Urban Wastewater Directive: national transposition is still open (DWA in the lobby register).
  • EU-MDR, LkSG, REACH: no new evidence.
