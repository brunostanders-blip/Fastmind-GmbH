Education and Culture Signals

1. Chinese companies in Germany: more hiring planned, labour law a hurdle (wirtschaftsticker, 11 Nov 2026)

  • New: A survey by the Chinese Chamber of Commerce in Germany and KPMG of 114 companies shows that 43% plan to hire more staff, 30% plan to invest more and 27% plan production in Germany.
  • Hurdle: 83% name labour costs and German labour law as an obstacle, ten percentage points more than last year; 65% name bureaucracy.
  • Europe: 57% run their European business from the German entity (last year 51%).
  • Relevance: Companies that hire in Germany and see labour law as a hurdle need managers who understand German work culture and co-determination. This matches Amy's cultural shield in the China to Germany direction.
  • Action: Offer an onboarding module on German work culture, works councils and leadership for Chinese managers in Germany, using the Chinese Chamber of Commerce in Germany as the channel.
  • Strategic question: yes

2. Bern University of Applied Sciences: next "Intercultural Training China" course in April 2027 (BFH course page)

  • New: The evening course runs again online on 28 Apr 2027 (17:30 to 19:30), registration deadline 21 Apr 2027, CHF 89 for external participants.
  • Relevance: A Swiss university offer at a price far below Amy's training prices. A Fastmind training in Switzerland will be measured against it.
  • Action: Position Amy's training not as an introduction but as a hands-on format with project support, which a two-hour course cannot provide.
