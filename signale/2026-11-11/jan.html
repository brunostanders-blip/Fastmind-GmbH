Robotics from China, Opportunities in DACH

Signals from the Chinese robotics and humanoid market for Germany, Austria and Switzerland, 11 November 2026.

China signals

Relevance high

1. Eight humanoids can be ordered in Germany, almost all from China, sold by German dealers

  • What is new:
    • Supply: A market overview counts 49 humanoids from 41 manufacturers. 14 can be bought, 13 of them come from China. Eight models can be delivered to Germany or the EU.
    • Models: Booster K1 and T1, Unitree R1 and G1, AgiBot X2 and A2 Lite, Dobot Atom Max and NAO V6. Prices range from EUR 9,000 to EUR 105,600 including VAT.
    • Dealers: reichelt elektronik supplies Unitree and AgiBot only to businesses, institutions and public authorities. Further dealers are Terra Robotics (Löhne), Unchained Robotics, Voelkner (Conrad group), Quadruped (Bergheim) and Funduino.
    • Measurement: Fraunhofer IPA in Stuttgart measured collision forces above 500 newtons on the Unitree G1. That is above the pain thresholds of ISO/TS 15066 for work next to people.
    • Security: Two vulnerabilities with full access via Bluetooth are documented for the G1; according to the source the latest one was closed by firmware on 1 September 2026.
    • Pilot: Rossmann has been testing a UBTECH Walker S2 at its Burgwedel logistics centre since 17 March 2026, delivered via Terra Robotics. There are no results yet.
  • Source: Humanoide Roboter Deutschland: 8 kaufbar, keiner fürs Zuhause, "Humanoid robots in Germany: 8 available, none for the home" (wortfilter.de, 4 Nov 2026): wortfilter.de, trade media. There is no Chinese source on this topic.
  • Conformity in the source: no; the source names only ISO/TS 15066, no CE marking and no Machinery Regulation.
  • Notified body and certificate number given: no.
  • Impact on DACH:
    • Sales: Companies can buy today; the route runs through a small number of German dealers.
    • Approval: The EU Machinery Regulation applies from 20 January 2027 (EUR-Lex). Dealers who import from China will then need evidence under the new law.
    • Risk: Measured collision forces above the threshold and documented vulnerabilities are a liability issue for operators (wortfilter.de).
  • Switzerland: For machinery, CE is recognised in Switzerland.
  • Opportunity for Fastmind: The dealers sell, but the source shows no evidence of conformity and no operating concept. Fastmind can give dealers and buyers the route through approval and accompany pilots.
  • Contribution to the strategic question: yes.
Relevance medium

2. Cainiao opens its global warehouse network to robotics makers and ships technology in three days

  • What is new:
    • Warehouse network: Cainiao (菜鸟), Alibaba's logistics arm, opens its warehouses worldwide to industry partners. Manufacturers can train robots there and test them in real operations.
    • Delivery service: The new service 科技出海三日达 ("technology abroad in three days") covers test units, volume delivery and operations abroad. It runs on 15 routes.
    • Figures: According to customs, China's robot exports from January to August were CNY 24.85 billion, an increase of 3.59 times.
    • Compliance: Cainiao says that in many projects customers ask about compliance first, and about time and price only after that.
    • Timeline: Cainiao expects commercial use of robots for simple warehouse tasks from 2027.
  • Source: 从送包裹到“造场景”，菜鸟押注具身智能第二增长曲线, "From delivering parcels to building use cases: Cainiao bets on embodied AI as its second growth curve" (Jiemian News, 10 Nov 2026): k.sina.com.cn, trade media.
  • Conformity in the source: no; the source speaks of compliance in general terms.
  • Notified body and certificate number given: no.
  • Impact on DACH:
    • Sales: Chinese manufacturers bring test units and volume goods to Europe faster; logistics is no longer a bottleneck.
    • Approval: Delivery in three days does not replace CE marking. The bottleneck moves to approval and local service.
    • Supply: DACH providers of warehouse technology can act as partners in the opened warehouses.
  • Switzerland: For machinery, CE is recognised in Switzerland.
  • Opportunity for Fastmind: Manufacturers that ship via Cainiao need a partner in DACH for approval and pilot customers. Fastmind can close this gap.
  • Contribution to the strategic question: yes.
Relevance medium

3. China's robotics makers court partners in Europe: largest delegation at Web Summit in Lisbon

  • What is new:
    • Web Summit: Web Summit 2026 opened in Lisbon on 9 November 2026. According to the organiser, the Chinese delegation is the largest so far.
    • Participants: Unitree, AgiBot, Galbot, Xiaomi, XPeng AeroHT and Pony.ai are represented.
    • Unitree: Founder Wang Xingxing said in Wuzhen on 3 November 2026 that Unitree wants to solve AI control of robots and is seeking cooperation worldwide for this.
  • Source:
    • 2026年网络峰会开幕, "Web Summit 2026 opens" (Xinhua via Quzhou Daily, 11 Nov 2026): finance.sina.com.cn, trade media; excerpt in the daily overview.
    • 王兴兴：宇树未来将重点攻克机器人大脑技术，拟面向全球寻求合作, "Wang Xingxing: Unitree will solve the robot brain and seeks cooperation worldwide" (Robot Global News after Caiwen, 3 Nov 2026): k.sina.com.cn, trade media.
    • Excerpt in the daily overview of 10 Nov 2026.
  • Conformity in the source: no.
  • Notified body and certificate number given: no.
  • Impact on DACH:
    • Sales: The manufacturers look for partners in Europe themselves and appear there together.
    • Supply: Unitree seeks partners for software and AI; this opens a door for DACH providers of control and sensor technology.
    • Risk: The source names no deals from Lisbon.
  • Switzerland: For machinery, CE is recognised in Switzerland.
  • Opportunity for Fastmind: The manufacturers can be approached in Europe this week. Fastmind can offer itself as the partner for DACH before fixed sales channels are in place.
  • Contribution to the strategic question: yes.

  • Approval: The sources on dealers, logistics and the trade show appearance name no CE marking. The EU Machinery Regulation applies from 20 January 2027 (EUR-Lex).
  • Safety: On the most tested model, collision forces above the threshold for work next to people were measured (wortfilter.de).
  • Logistics: Delivery and test operations abroad are becoming easier for Chinese manufacturers (Jiemian News).
  • Demand in DACH: New tenders for surgical robots or humanoids in Germany, Austria and Switzerland since 4 November 2026: sources checked, no new finding today (TED).
