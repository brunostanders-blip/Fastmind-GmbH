Regulation and compliance update

  • Machinery Regulation: Applies from 20.01.2027 (IHK Dortmund).
  • Duty holders: It names importers, distributors and authorised representatives explicitly (WEKA).
  • Standards list: The first list for the regulation is not yet published.
  • REACH, PFHxA: The ban for textiles in clothing has applied since 10.10.2026 (Beveridge & Diamond).
  • PFHxA, other textiles: They follow from 10.10.2027; construction textiles are exempt (IHK Ulm).
  • EU MDR and supply chain act: no new evidence.
