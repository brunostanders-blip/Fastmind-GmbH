Beite wastewater technology

1. Gießen wastewater plant (Mittelhessische Wasserbetriebe): renewal of the aeration in activated sludge basin 2

  • New: The tender covers 603 new membrane panel diffusers "or equivalent", plus 205 diffusers on existing pipes and a new stainless steel main air line (tendigo).
  • Deadline: Bids by 12.01.2027, 10:00; execution from 10.03. to 10.11.2027 (tendigo).
  • Award: The lowest price alone wins; variant bids are not allowed (tendigo).
  • Guaranteed values: At least 135 kg oxygen per hour as annual mean and 310 kg at peak are required (tendigo).
  • Relevance for Hanna (Beite): A pure price competition for diffusers "or equivalent" suits a lower-cost make, provided it proves the oxygen values.
  • Option: Offer Beite diffusers with data sheet, CE documents and proof of the oxygen values to plant builders bidding in Gießen before 12.01.2027.
  • Strategic question: yes.

2. Aischgrund wastewater plant (Abwasserverband Aischgrund, Diespeck): digester with agitator

  • New: Lot 4 covers a stainless steel digester of about 778 m³ usable volume including agitator, heating and foam trap; lot 5 the gas system (tendigo).
  • Deadline: Bids by 01.12.2026, completion by 30.11.2027 (tendigo).
  • Award: Technology and quality count 60 %, price 40 %; variant bids are not allowed (tendigo).
  • Gap: The summary does not name type or power of the agitator.
  • Relevance for Hanna (Beite): Here technology counts more than price, so an agitator with good documentation can score.
  • Option: The deadline is too short for an own bid; use the case as a model for digester tenders with a quality score and approach the contractor as a partner after the award.
  • Strategic question: yes.

Textiles (side field)

1. China opens an anti-dumping case against para-aramid filament yarn from the EU

  • New: China's Ministry of Commerce opened an anti-dumping investigation into para-aramid filament yarn from the EU on 06.11.2026 (Global Trade Alert).
  • Applicants: Sinochem High Performance Fiber Materials and Tayho Advanced Materials; the ministry cites price declines of more than 50 % since 2023 (MLex).
  • Context: Beijing presents the case as a response to EU trade defence cases (South China Morning Post).
  • Relevance for Hanna (textiles): High-performance fibres for technical textiles become the subject of mutual trade defence cases; this affects buyers on both sides.
  • Option: Use the situation as an opening for a supply chain conversation with DACH makers of technical textiles with a China link.
  • Strategic question: no.

Regulation and compliance update

  • Machinery Regulation: Until 19.01.2027 only the Machinery Directive applies, from 20.01.2027 only the Machinery Regulation (DGUV).
  • Self-learning safety functions: Safety components with self-evolving behaviour fall under Annex I Part A and generally need a third-party type examination (DGUV).
  • Standards list: A first list of harmonised standards under the Machinery Regulation could not be found today.
  • Product safety: Since 13.12.2024 manufacturers outside the EU need a responsible economic operator established in the EU (FPS Economy Belgium).
  • EU MDR, LkSG, REACH: no new evidence.

Strategic question

  • Wastewater, price award: The economical route is as an equivalent make in a plant builder's bid, provided the product proves the required guaranteed values (tendigo Gießen).

  • Robotics: It stays efficient to avoid self-learning safety functions or to plan a notified body early, because from 20.01.2027 they trigger a type examination (DGUV).
