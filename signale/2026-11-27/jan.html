Robotics from China, opportunities in DACH: 27 November 2026

Signals for Jan from the Chinese robotics and humanoid market.

China signals

1. Elite Robots from Shanghai reports CE-compliant mobile manipulators for factories, without a named standard or notified body (relevance: medium)
  • What is new: on 16 Nov 2026 Elite Robots (Shanghai, founded 2016) announced CE-compliant mobile manipulators of the ELITEMISSION range. They combine a cobot arm with an autonomous mobile platform (TNGlobal, PR Newswire).
  • Maker: ELITEMISSION is developed by the subsidiary Mission Robots Co., Ltd. (TNGlobal).
  • Use: machine loading and unloading, component handling, material transfer between production stages; industries automotive, electronics, semiconductors and new energy (TNGlobal).
  • Sales: customers in more than 50 countries through distributors, system integrators and partners; the release names no partner in Europe (TNGlobal).
  • Source: "Elite Robots Expands Global Mobile Automation Offering with CE-Compliant Mobile Manipulators", Shanghai, 16 Nov 2026, TNGlobal. Type: primary source, company press release via PR Newswire.
  • Conformity in the source: yes, only "CE-compliant"; the release names neither the Machinery Regulation nor EN ISO 10218, RED or any other standard (TNGlobal).
  • Notified body and certificate number named: no.
  • Approval: the Machinery Regulation (EU) 2023/1230 applies from 20 Jan 2027 (EUR-Lex); a buyer in DACH needs the declaration of conformity with the applied standards.
  • Sales: the target industries are automotive and electronics, the core customers of DACH integrators (TNGlobal).
  • Supply: the mobile manipulator is a complete system; DACH integrators supply the integration into the line.
  • Risk: without a named standard and notified body, the integrator carries the testing burden before acceptance.
  • Switzerland: machines with CE marking are recognised in Switzerland.
  • Opportunity for Fastmind: check Elite Robots' conformity documents for DACH integrators and broker a sales partner in DACH for Elite Robots.
  • Contributes to the strategic question: yes.
Already known
  • MIIT, five humanoid standards: known since 18.11.
  • New on this: the portal of the National Science and Technology Information Center confirms announcement No. 30 of 16 Nov 2026; the standards apply from publication, including electric joints and robot hands ("工信部发布5项人形机器人标准", MIIT issues five humanoid standards, 18 Nov 2026, ncsti.gov.cn).
  • Pudu Robotics, European headquarters in Neuss: known since 29.10.
  • New on this: Europe is the largest regional market, with average revenue growth of more than 60 % a year over the past two years; customers in DACH are Edeka and Denner (Switzerland, more than 200 CC1 cleaning robots); Pudu shows the D9 humanoid (Robotics & Automation News).
  • DACH suppliers for joints and hands of Chinese humanoids: known since 07.10.

Consequence for DACH

  • Approval: Chinese cobot systems enter the market as "CE-compliant" without a standard or notified body; the Machinery Regulation applies from 20 Jan 2027 (TNGlobal, EUR-Lex).
  • Sales: Pudu makes Europe its largest market and has Edeka and Denner as customers in DACH (Robotics & Automation News).
  • Supply: with the new standards China sets interfaces and test methods for joints and hands; DACH suppliers need to know these requirements (ncsti.gov.cn).
  • Risk: European researchers see durability problems in some parts of Chinese showcase models under heavy load (Euronews).
  • Demand in DACH: new tenders for surgical robots or humanoids: sources checked, no new find today.

Content angle

Thesis: since 16 Nov 2026 China standardises the joints and robot hands of its humanoids; anyone in DACH who wants to supply these parts must know the Chinese standard as well as the CE rules.
Evidence: MIIT announcement No. 30 puts standards for electric joints and robot hands into force at once (ncsti.gov.cn); gearboxes, motors, sensors and grippers are core business of German suppliers such as Schaeffler, Bosch, Schunk, Wittenstein and Neugart (Euronews).
