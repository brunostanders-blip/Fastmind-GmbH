Education and Culture Signals

1. CATL trains apprentices in Arnstadt and plans a battery technology certificate course with the Chamber of Commerce (IHK) South Thuringia (ingenieur.de, 5 Jan 2026)

  • New: From autumn 2026 the Chinese battery maker again starts apprenticeships in production, maintenance and logistics in Arnstadt, paying EUR 1,150 gross per month in an "intercultural" workplace (CATL careers).
  • Course: For second-year apprentices CATL plans a certificate course with an IHK certificate; a separate specialisation in the dual system is under review, and no start date is given.
  • Size: About 1,700 people work at the site, including about 30 apprentices.
  • Relevance: A Chinese employer in DACH does employer branding through dual vocational training. German apprentices and trainers work in a German-Chinese plant, and the sources mention no intercultural preparation.
  • Action: Offer CATL and IHK South Thuringia a bilingual module on working in German-Chinese teams for apprentices and trainers, as part of the planned course.
  • Strategic question: yes
  • Source: ingenieur.de (5 Jan 2026) and CATL careers page (as of 27 Nov 2026)

2. TH Wildau is developing a double degree in automation technology with Shanghai Dianji University (TH Wildau, 19 Dec 2025)

  • New: The university is pushing ahead with a double degree programme in automation technology with its partner university in Shanghai; it is still in development.
  • Setting: For 2026 a two-week summer school in July and a German-Chinese conference in Shanghai at the end of October were announced.
  • Relevance: Another double degree in a STEM subject is emerging; Chinese students will then come to Brandenburg for part of their studies. This matches Amy's support up to arrival and during studies.
  • Action: Offer the university now, while the programme is being developed, a package for preparation, arrival and study support for the students from Shanghai.
  • Strategic question: yes
  • Source: TH Wildau (19 Dec 2025)

Known since 06.11.: In October 2026 Nordhausen University of Applied Sciences opened a double degree in electrical engineering and computer science with Shanxi Datong University, with up to 40 students a year and the final year in Nordhausen (idw).

Known since 14.10.: With about 39,000 students, China remains the second most important country of origin of international students in Germany (Wissenschaft weltoffen 2026).

Strategic Question
