1. Yunji Robotics announces distributor contracts in Germany: hotel and hospital robots from Beijing look for sales partners within weeks

What is new: 云迹科技 Yunji Technology (Beijing, Hong Kong Stock Exchange 02670) published a business update on 16 October 2026. The company has recently entered Central Asia and Norway and "expects to sign robot sales orders with distributors in Norway, Greece, Germany and France within the next month". No volumes or amounts are given. As of 30 September 2026 its robots run in more than 45,000 hotels; the HDOS software platform has more than 5,000 customers (+230 % year on year). Robots online at the same time grew 20 to 25 % in the first nine months, completed tasks 25 to 30 %. Orders in progress: 1,000 robots for 野鸟 (delivery under way), 100 robots for hospitals (润翼), 2,000 robots under a framework agreement for delivery in 2027. The same day the chairwoman and the CEO committed not to sell shares for twelve months.

2. First Chinese surgical robot in a German hospital: "Shurui" from Beijing has operated in Munich Bogenhausen since May, paid per procedure

What is new: München Klinik Bogenhausen has used the Shurui single-port robotic system since 20 May 2026, the first hospital in Germany to do so (press release 24 June 2026). The manufacturer 北京术锐机器人 Beijing Surgerii Robotics reports the "first commercial installation in Germany" with pay-per-procedure billing; the statutory safety inspection (STK) was passed in one day. Use: thoracic and visceral surgery; on 25 September 2026 the hospital reported the world's first adrenal gland removal in prone position with the system. The team had travelled to Shanghai beforehand; a training console has stood in Bogenhausen since December 2025. The hospital cites CE approval in 2025; the company reports CE certification under the EU MDR dated 28 August 2025 for thoracic and abdominal procedures including paediatric surgery. In the first half of 2026 the company won a direct-sales tender at Vall d'Hebron University Hospital in Barcelona worth EUR 2.42 million. In July 2026 it filed its second application for a listing on Shanghai's STAR Market, planning to raise about CNY 700 million for capacity and overseas business.

Conformity named in the source: yes — CE (hospital: "CE approval 2025"; company: CE under EU MDR). Notified body and certificate number: no.

Consequence for DACH: Sales — the route into German hospitals is pay-per-use instead of purchase; this bypasses tenders above four million euros and the ten-year contracts of competitors (known since 29 Oct). Approval — CE under MDR plus the on-site STK were enough to operate; a publicly named importer and service partner in Germany is missing. Supply — training, on-site engineers and a hotline have to be built in Germany. Risk — patents around single-port systems (Intuitive SP); data flow of the console (GDPR); the IPO decides the financial strength for Europe.

Switzerland: Swissmedic, Swiss authorised representative, registration in swissdamed; hospitals with da Vinci fleets (Basel, known since 29 Oct) are the target group for a second system.

3. Vivantes Berlin buys a surgical robot for knee and hip: brand-open, MDR mandatory, price decides alone, bid deadline 4 November 2026

What is new: Vivantes, Germany's largest municipal hospital group, is tendering a mobile robotic assistance system for total hip and knee arthroplasty for Klinikum Am Urban (published 29 September 2026, bid deadline 4 November 2026). Scope: new device with an actively guided robotic arm (at least 6 axes, haptically limited; fully autonomous systems excluded), CT-based planning by the manufacturer, integration with the hospital information system and PACS, surgeon training, full maintenance over eight years with 95 % availability, consumables for more than 2,800 cases in eight years. Award: lowest price over eight-year life-cycle cost, quality 0 %. Mandatory: CE marking under MDR (EU) 2017/745 for hip and knee components, certified quality management system, average annual turnover of at least EUR 10 million, ISMS under ISO 27001, no data processing in third countries without legal basis, software bill of materials, declaration on the Russia embargo. Implants are procured separately; the bidder submits the list of approved implant systems. For comparison: Asklepios tendered a framework for knee robotics until 21 October 2026 with the Velys system (Johnson & Johnson) and the Attune implant specified as the make.

Source: Roboterassistiertes System Knie- & Hüftendoprothetik, Vivantes – Netzwerk für Gesundheit GmbH — "Robot-assisted system for knee and hip arthroplasty" (notice via tendigo): tendigo.de — contracting authority (primary source) via portal · Asklepios framework knee arthroplasty, make Velys, deadline 21 Oct 2026 (listing): auftragsmelder.com — contracting authority via portal · Chinese counterpart: Futurtec ORTHBOT X with CE MDR (known since 21 Aug): equalocean.com · 元化智能 Yuanhua Kunwu (EU certificate, more than 7,000 operations): stdaily.com.

Conformity named in the source: yes — CE under EU MDR as a minimum requirement. Notified body and certificate number: no.

Consequence for DACH: Sales — second brand-open orthopaedic tender within two weeks after Leipzig (known since 29 Oct), this time purchase instead of rental and hip included; Vivantes runs nine hospitals in Berlin, the award is a reference for the group. Approval — the MDR certificate is formally sufficient; in addition an importer under MDR Art. 13, registration under the German Medical Devices Act (MPDG), ISO 27001 proof. Risk — the Russia declaration and the ban on third-country data processing hit Chinese suppliers with cloud planning; turnover threshold EUR 10 million; the deadline is too short for a first-time bidder. The Asklepios procedure shows the other side: implant binding excludes third-party systems.

Switzerland: Swissmedic, Swiss authorised representative, swissdamed.

Opportunity for Fastmind: secure the Vivantes documents as the requirements profile for Chinese orthopaedic robotics (data processing in the EU, ISO 27001, implant approvals); position Futurtec or Yuanhua with a German importer for the next tenders; keep hospital groups that tender brand-open (Vivantes, Leipzig, Tübingen) as the target list.

4. China-EU fair in Chengdu: UBTECH signs with the Brussels association ChinaEU, experts name proof of conformity as the bottleneck for Chinese robots in Europe

What is new: At the 19th China-EU cooperation fair (欧洽会, Chengdu, 22–23 October 2026; about 300 companies from more than 20 European countries, more than 160 letters of intent according to the organiser) 优必选 UBTECH and the association ChinaEU (Brussels) signed a memorandum of understanding: ChinaEU is to introduce partners in Europe, assess opportunities and explain the rules, with a focus on manufacturing and logistics; UBTECH contributes its European distributor network. Luigi Gambardella (ChinaEU) at the industry meeting: the bottleneck for Chinese robot makers in Europe is "not the technology but how to prove conformity"; tests done in China often cannot be used as evidence. He pointed to the Machinery Regulation from January 2027 and proposed a joint initiative of CEN/CENELEC and SAC on the recognition of test results. Figures from the report: China's industrial robot exports to the EU in 2025 were 3.22 times those of 2021, in the first three quarters of 2026 almost CNY 10 billion (+16.9 %); EU new installations in 2025 −11 % to 60,500, Germany below 25,000 (−8 %), Europe's largest market. 成都卡诺普 CRP Robot (Chengdu, industrial and welding robots) names more than 20 partners in 13 European countries.

Source: 中欧智能制造与机器人合作：在新型互补中寻找增量 — "China-EU: intelligent manufacturing and robotics, growth from new complementarity" (第一财经, 23 Oct 2026): finance.sina.com.cn — trade press · 中欧数字协会与优必选建立战略合作伙伴关系 — "ChinaEU and UBTECH form strategic partnership" (同花顺, 25 Oct 2026): 10jqka.com.cn — company release (primary source, reprint) · same at eetrend.com · 双向投资赋能中欧产业融合！第十九届欧洽会在蓉开幕 — "19th China-EU fair opens in Chengdu" (澎湃新闻, 22 Oct 2026): thepaper.cn — organiser report · European companies put China tech to work (Xinhua via Manila Times, 28 Oct 2026): manilatimes.net — news agency.

Conformity named in the source: yes — EU Machinery Regulation and ISO 10218 (2025 revision), plus the EU AI Omnibus. Notified body and certificate number: no.

Consequence for DACH: Approval — a European association states publicly what Fastmind offers as a service: translating test results from China into EU conformity. Sales — UBTECH (DACH exclusively via Terra Robotics, known since 29 Oct) gains Brussels network access; Germany, with fewer than 25,000 new installations, remains Europe's largest robot market but is shrinking. Supply — CRP Robot says it is looking for European partners for technical support and after-sales. Risk — the EU is negotiating market access at the same time (trade and investment consultation mechanism, October 2026).

Switzerland: CE recognised; ISO 10218 applies through the SN adoption.
