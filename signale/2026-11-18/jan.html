Robotics from China, opportunities in DACH: 18 November 2026

Signals for Jan from the Chinese robotics and humanoid market.

China signals

1. Edge Medical's single-port surgical robot is used for the first time in European thoracic surgery (relevance: medium)
  • What is new: the single-port system SP1000 by 精锋医疗 Edge Medical (Shenzhen) was used at Hospital Memorial Publio Cordón in Madrid, the first use in European thoracic surgery.
  • Scope: two lung procedures on the same day, through an incision of about 4 cm, lasting about 20 and about 50 minutes.
  • Source: „精锋单孔机器人完成欧洲胸外科首次临床应用！“ (Edge Medical single-port robot completes first clinical use in European thoracic surgery), 17 Nov 2026, 星岛环球网. Type: company statement (Edge Medical, HKEX 02675) distributed by a news portal.
  • Conformity in the source: yes, the source names CE certification, without reference to the EU MDR.
  • Notified body and certificate number named: no.
  • Approval: with a CE mark the SP1000 can also be offered to hospitals in Germany and Austria. The notified body remains open in the source.
  • Sales: no European sales partner is named for the SP1000. For Edge Medical's multi-port system MP1000, Dornier MedTech (Weßling near Munich) runs a product page for Germany today (Dornier MedTech).
  • Switzerland: to sell to Swiss hospitals, Edge Medical needs a Swiss authorised representative and registration in swissdamed; Swissmedic supervises the market.
  • Opportunity for Fastmind: assess thoracic and general surgery in DACH as a field for the SP1000 and connect Edge Medical with hospitals and its existing DACH distribution.
  • Contributes to the strategic question: yes.
2. China puts five humanoid standards into force, including joint modules and robot hands (relevance: medium)
  • What is new: the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology (MIIT) published five humanoid robot standards with Announcement No. 30 of 2026 on 16 Nov 2026. They apply from publication.
  • Content: intelligence capability levels, electrically driven integrated joints, robot hands, building of training grounds and management of training data.
  • Joints: the integrated joint standard sets interfaces, performance values and test methods for torso and leg joints.
  • Source: „工信部发布5项人形机器人标准，涉及智能化能力分级、灵巧手及训练数据等“ (MIIT publishes five humanoid standards covering intelligence levels, robot hands and training data), 17 Nov 2026, The Paper, reprint NetEase; the MIIT page could not be retrieved. Type: trade media citing the MIIT standards committee, confirmed by two reprints.
  • Conformity in the source: no, these are Chinese standards with no reference to CE or EN ISO 10218.
  • Notified body and certificate number named: no.
  • Supply: DACH makers of joints, gears, sensors and grippers that supply Chinese humanoid makers will be measured against these standards: interfaces and test values are now fixed.
  • Approval: for export to Europe the standards replace neither CE nor the EU Machinery Regulation from 20 Jan 2027 (EUR-Lex).
  • Switzerland: machines with CE marking are recognised in Switzerland.
  • Opportunity for Fastmind: offer DACH suppliers a comparison between the Chinese joint and hand standards and European evidence.
  • Contributes to the strategic question: no.
Already known

  • Dornier MedTech and Edge Medical: distribution of the MP1000 in Germany and Austria; known since 31.10., the product page still shows the offer today (Dornier MedTech).

  • Agile Robots: funding of more than 500 million US dollars and more production in Germany; known since 12.11.

Consequence for DACH

  • Approval: Chinese surgical robots with CE marks are used in European hospitals, now also in thoracic surgery (星岛环球网).
  • Supply: China fixes test values for joints and robot hands; DACH suppliers must serve both sets of standards (The Paper).
  • Demand in DACH: new tenders for surgical robots or humanoids: sources checked, no new find today.

Content angle

Thesis: China standardises the joints and hands of its humanoids, Europe tests the whole as a machine; anyone supplying or selling in DACH now needs both kinds of evidence.
Evidence: five MIIT standards apply since 16 Nov 2026, including joint modules and robot hands (The Paper); the EU Machinery Regulation applies from 20 Jan 2027 (EUR-Lex).
