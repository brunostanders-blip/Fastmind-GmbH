China Signals

Relevance medium

1. Chinese memory chips already run in industrial robots — Ingenic confirms the use, not yet in humanoids

  • What is new: Chipmaker 君正股份 Ingenic (Beijing, 300223.SZ) replied on its investor platform on 2 November 2026: its memory chips are “already used in industrial robots, hotel service robots and similar fields, not yet in humanoid robots”. The company is developing memory products for on-device AI. The reply says nothing about prices, lead times or overseas customers.
  • Source: 君正股份：公司存储芯片已在工业机器人等领域实现应用，目前尚未应用于人形机器人 — “Ingenic: the company’s memory chips are used in industrial robots and other fields, not yet in humanoid robots” (财闻 via Sina Finance, 2 Nov 2026): finance.sina.com.cn — trade media quoting the company reply.
  • Conformity in the source: no.
  • Notified body and certificate number named: no.
  • Consequence for DACH: Supply — robotics SMEs in DACH short of memory chips have a Chinese source with documented robotics use. Approval — for components in machinery, the conformity of the end product counts; radio modules fall under RED with EN 18031, connected controllers under the Cyber Resilience Act from 2027. Risk — supply-chain and export-control questions on semiconductors must be checked before the first order.
  • Switzerland: For machinery with these components: CE recognised.
  • Opportunity for Fastmind: A sourcing dossier “memory and control chips from China for robotics SMEs”: makers, proof of use, distribution route into the EU, RED and Cyber Resilience Act checkpoints.
  • Contributes to the strategic question: no.
Relevance low

2. Nuremberg shows 200 inventions from China — including a humanoid inspection robot for power grids

  • What is new: At the International Trade Fair for Inventions in Nuremberg (iENA), which ended on 2 November 2026 after three days, Xinhua reports that “约200项” (about 200) of “近600项” (nearly 600) inventions came from China. The China Electric Power Research Institute (中国电力科学研究院) showed a humanoid robot for autonomous inspection of power facilities. Other exhibits come from medicine and rehabilitation.
  • Source: 综述｜中国创新成果亮相德国纽伦堡国际发明展 — “Overview: Chinese innovations at the International Inventions Fair in Nuremberg” (Xinhua via Sina, 2 Nov 2026): k.sina.com.cn — trade media.
  • Conformity in the source: no.
  • Notified body and certificate number named: no.
  • Consequence for DACH: Distribution — Chinese institutes show prototypes in Germany before a sales route exists; grid operators and municipal utilities are the natural buyers. Approval — an inspection robot in substations needs CE under machinery law, from 20 January 2027 under the EU Machinery Regulation. Risk — power grids are critical infrastructure; Chinese technology there is under political scrutiny.
  • Switzerland: CE recognised.
  • Opportunity for Fastmind: The list of Chinese exhibitors is a pool of inventors looking for a partner for a first pilot in DACH. Entry works better through logistics and industry than through critical infrastructure.
  • Contributes to the strategic question: yes.

7. China’s first humanoid standards are recommendations — the safety standard is still missing

  • What is new: The six national standards apply from 1 April 2027 and are recommended standards, not a condition for market access. The standard “Safety requirements Part 1: general safety” is still in the standards plan, with no date.
  • Source: 首批人形机器人领域国家标准发布 — “First national standards for humanoid robots published” (China Daily, 16 Oct 2026): caijing.chinadaily.com.cn — trade media · 人形机器人首批国标：先统一算法，再谈安全认证 — “First humanoid standards: unify algorithms first, then safety certification” (aiman.world): aiman.world — trade media.
  • Conformity in the source: no.
  • Notified body and certificate number named: no.
  • Consequence for DACH: Approval — Chinese standards compliance does not replace safety evidence under EN ISO 10218 and the EU Machinery Regulation.
  • Switzerland: CE recognised.
  • Opportunity for Fastmind: The gap between a Chinese performance standard and the European safety obligation is the core of every approval consultation.
  • Contributes to the strategic question: no.

Consequences for DACH

  • Approval: None of the reports names CE, a notified body or a certificate number. China’s own standards govern performance, not safety. For every humanoid and every hand from China, the route to DACH starts with EN ISO 10218 and the EU Machinery Regulation from 20 January 2027.
  • Distribution: UBTECH and Linkerbot rely on local partners in Europe instead of their own companies. Whoever brings approval and pilot customers becomes the partner.
  • Supply: Mass-produced joint modules (Beijing) and memory chips with robotics use (Ingenic) widen the sources for DACH robotics SMEs.
  • Risk: Semiconductors and power grids are politically sensitive; check export control, critical infrastructure and patents (tactile sensing) before the first order.
  • Dates in the next 12 weeks: MEDICA Düsseldorf 16–19 Nov 2026 (ilt.fraunhofer.de) · SPS Nuremberg 24–26 Nov 2026 (uitholland.eu) · humanoid robot fair Shanghai 9–11 Dec 2026 (robotexhibition.cn).

Content Angle

Thesis: China’s humanoid makers are not looking for buyers in Europe but for partners on the ground — the seat at the table goes to whoever brings approval and the first pilot customer.

Evidence: UBTECH has a Brussels association support partner matching and regulation (embodied-ai.eetrend.com); Linkerbot wants to expand through “local partners” and strengthen ties with German industry (stock.10jqka.com.cn). Neither report mentions CE.

Strategic Question

In DACH, the IAOW under Hans-Peter Friedrich is seeking Chinese robotics partners and visited Linkerbot in Beijing for that purpose (10jqka); MEDICA in Düsseldorf (16–19 Nov) and SPS in Nuremberg (24–26 Nov) are the next meeting points (Fraunhofer ILT, uitholland.eu). On the Chinese side, UBTECH seeks a local partner network in the EU (eetrend) and Linkerbot seeks local partners for distribution (10jqka). About 200 Chinese inventions in Nuremberg show that more suppliers are looking for a way into Germany (k.sina.com.cn).
