Robotics from China, Opportunities in DACH

Signals from the Chinese robotics and humanoid market for Germany, Austria and Switzerland, 6 November 2026.

China signals

Relevance high

1. China blocks SoftBank from buying ABB's robotics business in China

  • What is new:
    • Prohibition: ABB (Zurich) confirmed on 5 Nov 2026 that China's office for the security review of foreign investment has prohibited SoftBank's purchase of ABB's robotics business in China.
    • Deal: The sale of the whole robotics division to SoftBank was agreed in October 2025, at an enterprise value of USD 5.375 bn.
    • Status: According to ABB, both companies remain "in close dialogue"; no termination of the overall deal has been announced.
    • Procedure: The block came from the investment security review, not from antitrust. The authority gave no reasons.
  • Source:
    • Trade media: 日本软银以54亿美元收购ABB机器人的计划受挫, "SoftBank's plan to buy ABB Robotics for USD 5.4 bn stalls" (21st Century Business Herald, 5 Nov 2026): 21jingji.com
    • Primary source: company statement on abb.com; the page could not be retrieved today, its content is confirmed by two reprints.
    • Second reprint: The Japan Times
  • Conformity in the source: no.
  • Notified body and certificate number given: no.
  • Impact on DACH:
    • Risk: Robotics business in China is now a matter of investment security. Anyone selling or buying a robotics unit with a Chinese subsidiary must expect a review that carves out the China part.
    • Sales: For ABB customers in China and in DACH it remains open who will run the China business.
  • Switzerland: A Swiss group is directly affected. For machinery, CE is recognised in Switzerland.
  • Opportunity for Fastmind: Advice for DACH mid-sized companies with robotics or automation subsidiaries in China: plan a sale, stake or partnership so that the Chinese review is considered early.
  • Contributes to the strategic question: yes.
Relevance medium

2. Chinese surgical robots push abroad: exports in H1 2026 more than quadrupled

  • What is new:
    • Exports: According to a report from the 140th Canton Fair (3 Nov 2026), China exported surgical robots worth CNY 4.8 bn in H1 2026, "同比增长3.3倍" (an increase of 3.3 times). Export markets rose from 23 to 49.
    • 精锋医疗 Edge Medical (HKEX 02675): approvals in 37 jurisdictions for its multi-port robot; H1 2026 revenue up 113.8 % to CNY 3.19 bn.
    • 微创机器人 MicroPort MedBot (HKEX 02252): overseas revenue of the Toumai system up 450 % in H1 2026.
    • 哈尔滨思哲睿: laparoscopic robot Kangduo, in Europe since 2025, training centres in France and Poland in 2026.
    • 迈瑞医疗 Mindray: is developing its own surgical robot.
  • Source: 国产手术机器人"出海"探路, "Chinese surgical robots explore the way abroad" (Economic Observer via Sina Finance, 5 Nov 2026): finance.sina.com.cn, trade media.
  • Conformity in the source: no. The source names neither CE nor the EU MDR, only "approvals" in jurisdictions.
  • Notified body and certificate number given: no.
  • Impact on DACH:
    • Approval: In DACH only CE under the EU MDR counts, with indications that match the tender.
    • Sales: The manufacturers are building training centres in the EU, but none in DACH. The report does not mention Germany.
    • Risk: The figures come from company statements and a trade fair report, not from customs statistics.
  • Switzerland: For medical devices a Chinese manufacturer needs a Swiss authorised representative (CH-REP), registration in swissdamed and notification to Swissmedic.
  • Opportunity for Fastmind: Set up a training and reference centre in DACH for a Chinese surgical robot maker, with a hospital, an approval check and a sales partner.
  • Contributes to the strategic question: yes.
Relevance medium

3. Hamburg awards a cardiac surgery robot system to the da Vinci 5 without competition

  • What is new:
    • Award: University Medical Center Hamburg-Eppendorf (UKE) intends to award a multi-port robotic surgery system for minimally invasive cardiac and aortic surgery to Intuitive Surgical Deutschland in a negotiated procedure without prior call, type da Vinci 5, term 7 years.
    • Dates: notice dated 5 Nov 2026, published 6 Nov 2026.
    • Justification: technical. Required are approval for cardiac use, instruments as class III medical devices under the EU MDR, seven degrees of freedom, tremor reduction, analytics, training and a simulator.
  • Source: notice "Multiport Roboter-assistiertes Operationssystem" by UKE: ted.europa.eu, primary source (contracting authority).
  • Conformity in the source: yes. The notice cites Regulation (EU) 2017/745 (MDR), class III.
  • Notified body and certificate number given: no.
  • Impact on DACH:
    • Approval: Cardiac surgery stays closed to Chinese systems as long as none has CE approval for this indication.
    • Comparison: Cornerstone Sentire is approved under the EU MDR for general, gynaecological, thoracic and urological procedures (known since 1 Sep).
    • Sales: Where a hospital tenders brand-open there is a chance; for special indications there is none.
  • Switzerland: For medical devices the route is CH-REP, swissdamed and Swissmedic.
  • Opportunity for Fastmind: Sort DACH tenders by indication and show Chinese manufacturers where their CE approval is sufficient: urology, gynaecology and general surgery rather than cardiac surgery.
  • Contributes to the strategic question: no.
Relevance medium

4. Bosch turns its semiconductors into a separate division and names humanoids as a target

  • What is new:
    • Division: Bosch announced on 3 Nov 2026 that it will run its semiconductor business as a separate division from 1 Jan 2028; the target is EUR 5 bn in semiconductor revenue by 2030.
    • Fields: motion control for humanoid robots, power components for AI data centres, industrial automation.
    • Volume: Since 2021 Bosch has shipped more than 60 million silicon carbide chips. The report does not mention China.
  • Source: 官宣！博世半导体独立, "Official: Bosch semiconductors become independent" (Jinri Xinwen via Sina Finance, 5 Nov 2026): finance.sina.com.cn, trade media.
  • Conformity in the source: no.
  • Notified body and certificate number given: no.
  • Impact on DACH:
    • Supply: A DACH group is aiming chips at humanoids, whose largest makers are in China.
    • Demand: Unitree founder Wang Xingxing named chip capacity one of the five core resources of the future on 5 Nov (k.sina.com.cn).
  • Switzerland: CE recognised.
  • Opportunity for Fastmind: Open access to Chinese humanoid makers for DACH component suppliers, here for motion control chips.
  • Contributes to the strategic question: yes.

5. Humanoid Europe in Munich, second day

  • What is new: The congress for European and Chinese humanoid robotics runs on 5 and 6 Nov 2026 in Munich. Unitree and Galbot are listed as event partners on the organiser's site.
  • Source: organiser: humanoideurope2026.com, primary source.
  • Conformity in the source: no. The programme includes a track on certification and European compliance.
  • Notified body and certificate number given: no.
  • Impact on DACH: Sales: Chinese makers and DACH suppliers are in the same place today.

Impact on DACH

  • Approval: Only the UKE notice cites the EU MDR. For Chinese surgical robots, the approved indication decides whether they can bid in DACH at all.
  • Sales: Chinese surgical robot makers are building training centres in France and Poland, not in DACH. For special indications the DACH market stays with Intuitive.
  • Supply: Bosch is aiming its chips at humanoids. Schaeffler names humanoid robotics as a growth area while cutting 3,460 jobs in Europe (Humanoids Daily).
  • Risk: China reviews robotics business for investment security and has stopped the China part of the ABB sale (21jingji.com).
  • Humanoid Europe: Munich, 5 to 6 Nov 2026 (humanoideurope2026.com).
  • China International Import Expo: Shanghai, 5 to 10 Nov 2026 (k.sina.com.cn).
  • MEDICA: Düsseldorf, 16 to 19 Nov 2026 (ilt.fraunhofer.de).
  • SPS: Nuremberg, 24 to 26 Nov 2026 (uitholland.eu).
  • Humanoid fair Shanghai: 9 to 11 Dec 2026 (robotexhibition.cn).

Content angle

Thesis: Robotics has become a question of control: China will not let ABB's robotics business go to SoftBank, and in DACH the approved indication decides whether a surgical robot from China may bid at all.
  • China: The investment security office prohibited the purchase of ABB's robotics business in China (21jingji.com).
  • Exports: China's surgical robot exports rose to CNY 4.8 bn in H1 2026 (finance.sina.com.cn).
  • DACH: UKE awards cardiac surgery to the da Vinci 5 without competition because it requires class III instruments under the EU MDR for this indication (ted.europa.eu).

Strategic question

  • DACH seeks China: Humanoid Europe in Munich brings Unitree and Galbot together with European companies today (humanoideurope2026.com).
  • DACH suppliers: Schaeffler is growing its humanoid business with customers in China (Humanoids Daily).
  • China seeks Europe: Edge Medical, 思哲睿 and MicroPort MedBot are looking for markets and training sites in Europe, so far without a centre in DACH (Sina Finance).
  • Limit: The ABB case shows that China reviews sales of robotics business abroad; cooperations need a structure that takes this into account (21jingji.com).
