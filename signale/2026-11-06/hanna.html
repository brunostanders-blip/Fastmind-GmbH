Market signals

Robotics

New 1. The list of standards under the old Machinery Directive ends on 20 Jan 2027: Implementing Decision (EU) 2026/2015 of 4 Sep 2026 repeals the previous list decision 2023/1586 with effect from that day; the first list under the Machinery Regulation is not yet published
  • New: Decision (EU) 2026/2015 updates the harmonised standards under Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC and appeared in the Official Journal on 7 Sep 2026.
  • Repeal: It repeals Decision (EU) 2023/1586 with effect from 20 Jan 2027 (EUR-Lex, umwelt-online).
  • Last list: Swissmem calls 2026/2015 probably the last publication under the old directive.
  • Machinery Regulation: The Commission aims to adopt the first decision under Regulation (EU) 2023/1230 by the end of 2026; standards that do not fully cover the new requirements are to be listed with restrictions (Swissmem, IBF Solutions).
  • Relevance for Hanna (Robotics): An EU declaration of conformity from a Chinese robot or cobot maker that relies on standards from the old list loses its presumption of conformity on 20 Jan 2027. As long as the new list is missing, DACH dealers and integrators face a gap in eleven weeks.
  • Action option: Offer Chinese makers a check of their declaration of conformity against the Machinery Regulation; give DACH importers a checklist of what the declaration must contain from 20 Jan 2027.
  • Strategic question: yes.

Beite wastewater technology

New 2. Bergisch-Rheinischer Wasserverband renews the aeration of activated-sludge group 1 at the Solingen-Ohligs plant: fine-bubble membrane aeration for four basins, three oil-free compressors, removal of the old mixers; deadline 17 Dec 2026, 15:00
  • New: Published 3 Nov 2026, open procedure under VOB, one lot.
  • Scope (portal summary of the tender documents): about 130 m² of aerator area per basin 1.1 to 1.4, stainless steel air pipes, three compressors with frequency converters (1,100 to 3,400 Nm³/h each), efficiency guarantee of at most 1.05 kW/(Nm³/min), control system as a mandatory make, execution roughly 18 Jan 2027 to 14 Mar 2028.
  • Suitability: reference for a retrofit at a plant of at least 10,000 PE in the last five years, turnover at least 1.5 times the bid value, certified welders; the bidder must state make and type of the aerators.
  • Award: lowest price including maintenance as present value; according to the portal the documents are not fully clear on this point.
  • Relevance for Hanna (Beite): This is the construction project for aeration at the same association that is currently tendering the planning in Mettmann (known since 2 Nov). Beite cannot bid alone (reference, turnover), but can be the named make in the bid of a German plant builder; price and energy guarantee decide.
  • Action option: By the end of November approach plant builders doing aeration retrofits in North Rhine-Westphalia and offer Beite's aerators with energy values as the make.
  • Strategic question: yes.
  • Source: tendigo: KW Ohligs, aeration renewal
New 3. New treatment plant of the Ahlbachgruppe (Bavaria): blower station with three screw blowers and aeration technology as one contract, deadline 7 Dec 2026, 08:00; no variant bids
  • New: Published 4 Nov 2026, open procedure, no lots; delivery and installation of the blower station, aeration elements, piping, commissioning with performance proof and four years of maintenance; installation roughly 13 Aug 2027 to 3 Oct 2028.
  • Conditions: three references only on request, performance bond of 10 % from German branches, warranty security of 5 %.
  • Open points: the portal names the contracting authority inconsistently (BayernGrund GmbH and Zweckverband Ahlbachgruppe Hoch 5); mixers are not mentioned.
  • Relevance for Hanna (Beite): A new build with aeration bundled with blowers. Without variant bids only the bidder's main offer counts; the bond requires a bank with a German branch, so a DACH partner is needed.
  • Action option: Approach blower suppliers and plant builders in Bavaria that could offer Beite's aerators as part of their package.
  • Strategic question: yes.
  • Source: tendigo: Ahlbachgruppe, blower station and aeration
