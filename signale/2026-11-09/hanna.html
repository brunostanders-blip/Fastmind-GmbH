Market signals

Robotics

New 1. Humanoid Europe in Munich: Swiss start-up Embodied AI presents AIR, a wheeled robot built to work beside factory staff without a cage, and plans to reach CE marking only in 2027
  • New: Embodied AI (Lausanne, EPFL spin-off, 25 staff) presents the two-armed wheeled robot AIR at Humanoid Europe (Humanoids Daily, 6 Nov 2026).
  • Orders: They are to open after the unveiling.
  • CE route: The company plans CE marking for 2027 and links it to Machinery Regulation (EU) 2023/1230 from 20 Jan 2027; without the mark the robot cannot be sold as a finished machine in the EU (Humanoids Daily).
  • Gaps: The report names no notified body, standards, price or delivery date; figures on pilot customers and success rates come from the company and are unverified.
  • Relevance for Hanna (Robotics): Even a provider from the DACH region, appearing at the same conference as Unitree and Galbot, brings its robot to market with CE under the Machinery Regulation only in 2027.
  • Benchmark for Chinese makers: Whoever wants to supply DACH factories in 2027 needs conformity under the new regulation, not under the old directive.
  • Action option: Use AIR as a comparison case in talks with Chinese humanoid makers: set their CE timeline under the Machinery Regulation against that of the Swiss competitor.
  • Strategic question: yes.

Beite wastewater technology

New 2. Water and wastewater association Gotha und Landkreisgemeinden: new treatment plant for 520 population equivalents in Gamstädt (Nesse-Apfelstädt) with mechanical equipment, pumping station and pipes as one lot; deadline 18 Nov 2026
  • New: Published on 19 Oct 2026 via evergabe-online (ID 897328), one lot, construction works (VergabeHero).
  • Scope: treatment basin with integrated sludge storage (about 23 × 7 × 5 m) including mechanical equipment, shaft pumping station 7 l/s, about 400 m of pressure pipe, operations building, plus mechanical, electrical, telecontrol and process control technology.
  • Gaps: Aeration, mixers, award criteria and execution period are not in the readable text; the deadline time is inconsistent on the portal (10:30 or 01:00).
  • Relevance for Hanna (Beite): A small plant where all mechanical equipment sits in the construction contract. Beite can only come in as supplier to the bidding contractor or plant builder; whether mixers or aerators are needed only the bill of quantities will show.
  • Action option: Check the tender documents on evergabe-online and, if the technology fits, approach regional plant builders in Thuringia with Beite's data sheets.
  • Strategic question: yes.

Customer outreach ideas

  • "Your humanoid with CE in 2027, not later": Approach Chinese humanoid makers who want to supply DACH with the timeline of the Swiss competitor AIR and offer a gap analysis of their documents under the Machinery Regulation.
  • "Who won Neckarsulm?": After the bid validity period identify the winning bidder for the 8 mixers and present Beite's mixers for the next fourth-stage projects.
  • "Small plant, one supplier for the mechanical equipment": Offer contractors bidding for Gamstädt a package of mixer and aerator with German data sheets.

Strategic question

  • Robotics: The economic route is to align conformity directly with the Machinery Regulation, because without CE marking no sale as a finished machine is possible and even DACH providers plan for 2027 (Humanoids Daily).
  • Standards: As long as the first list of standards under the Machinery Regulation is missing, the documentation must prove the new requirements directly (IBF Solutions).
  • Wastewater: The efficient route is supplying a German bidder, because municipalities tender even small plants including mechanical equipment as one lot (VergabeHero Gamstädt).
  • Fibres: no answer from today's sources.

Recommendations for Jan

  • Recommendation: Follow the award in Neckarsulm and approach the winning bidder as a partner for Beite's mixers, because the deadline ends today, the award is on price only and more plants from 150,000 PE must retrofit the fourth treatment stage.
  • Recommendation: Have the Gamstädt documents checked for mixers and aeration before 18 Nov, because the publicly readable text does not list the mechanical equipment in detail.
  • Recommendation: Add Embodied AI's AIR to the checklist "declaration of conformity from 20 Jan 2027" as a comparison case, because it shows how a competitor communicates its CE timeline under the Machinery Regulation openly.
