Amy: Education and Culture Signals

New developments in education and culture between China and Germany, Austria and Switzerland, 9 November 2026.

Fields: Education (university cooperation under security review, German-Chinese university networks) and Culture (integration after an acquisition).
Key finding: University cooperation with China is being re-measured. The federal government has set up a National Platform for Research Security, rejections of applications from Chinese researchers and students are rising, and BMFTR and HRK are briefing universities on the legal framework in November. On the Chinese side, the network of German-Chinese universities keeps growing. On the culture side, the EU gave the last approval on 3 November for JD.com's takeover of Ceconomy (MediaMarkt): around 50,000 employees in Europe get a Chinese majority owner.
Context note: Statements about Amy's positioning come from the document "Fastmind Dual-Gateway", not from own research.

Education and Culture Signals

new

1. Germany sets up a National Platform for Research Security; in 2025 about 200 of some 2,300 research applications and about 200 of some 9,000 study applications from China were rejected (Euronews, 2 Nov 2026)

  • New: The platform met for the first time in mid-October 2026. It advises on risk assessment, protective measures, critical partners and national risk analyses, funded by BMFTR.
  • Numbers with consequences: According to WDR, rejections rose compared with previous years; only applications from Iran are rejected more often. The debate was triggered by a case at the CISPA Helmholtz Center in Saarbrücken.
  • Context: That risk assessment and research security belong to China competence alongside language and culture has been known since 07.09.; the federal platform as a permanent body is new.
  • Relevance: Universities admitting Chinese students must accompany applications more carefully. Anyone placing students from China in Germany needs to know the review in advance and prepare documents accordingly.
  • Action option: Add preparation for the review process (field of study, documents, expectations) to the support for students and families in China, and offer universities a clean, documented pre-selection.
  • Strategic question: yes
  • Source: Euronews: Spionage, wie Chinas Agenten deutsche Hochschulen unterwandern (02.11.2026)
new

2. BMFTR and HRK offer universities an update on the legal framework for science cooperation with China on 26 Nov 2026, online, registration by 24 Nov 2026 (C-NET RNR, 4 Nov 2026)

  • New: Next session of the series "Framework conditions for successful cooperation with China", 9:30 to 11:00 via WebEx, on current developments, legal challenges and laws in force in China.
  • Target group: Universities and science, funding and intermediary organisations; places are limited, registration via the China team at DLR.
  • Relevance: Participants are exactly the international offices and cooperation managers Amy addresses. The series delivers law, not support for people.
  • Action option: Take part through a partner university or intermediary organisation and use the questions raised as an opener with international offices.
  • Strategic question: yes
  • Source: C-NET RNR: BMFTR-HRK, Rahmenbedingungen für eine erfolgreiche Kooperation mit China (04.11.2026)
new

3. The China competence network Ruhr Niederrhein meets in Duisburg on 18 Nov 2026: "Cooperation under review", registration by 15 Nov 2026 (C-NET RNR, 5 Nov 2026)

  • New: Network meeting from 10:30 to 14:15 in Duisburg on EU-China relations, Germany's role and cooperation in science and business, with keynotes, experience reports and news on the network's future.
  • Context: The network has been known since 07.09. (China Day in Bochum); the Duisburg date with a registration deadline is new.
  • Relevance: A meeting in North Rhine-Westphalia, close to Düsseldorf, with universities and companies currently reviewing their China cooperation. This is the circle that needs support and integration.
  • Action option: Sign up by 15 Nov and bring two offers: preparation and arrival of Chinese students, and China competence for teaching staff and employees.
  • Strategic question: yes
  • Source: C-NET RNR: Kooperationen auf dem Prüfstand, Netzwerkveranstaltung am 18. November 2026 in Duisburg (05.11.2026)
new

4. Tenth China-Germany Forum on integrating education, science and industry in Hangzhou: more than 50 universities from both countries, launch of a "Munich Institute of Digital Innovation" at Zhejiang University of Science and Technology (Hangzhou municipal government, 20 Oct 2026)

  • New: The forum took place on 16 Oct 2026 with more than 200 participants, over ten companies and a network of more than 60 member universities from China and Germany. The new institute is the university's third cooperative institution approved by China's Ministry of Education.
  • Open: The report names no German partner for the institute and no student numbers. A youth initiative on intelligent manufacturing and AI was also launched.
  • Context: This university's double degrees with HTW Dresden have been known since 30.10.
  • Relevance: An existing network of more than 60 universities with industry ties is a channel for students moving to Germany.
  • Action option: Identify the institute's German partner and the forum's German members and offer them support for students from application to arrival.
  • Strategic question: no
  • Source: Hangzhou Municipal People's Government: China-Germany forum advances collaboration in education, science, industry (20.10.2026)
new

5. EU approves JD.com's takeover of Ceconomy (MediaMarkt, Saturn) with conditions on 3 Nov 2026; closing in the coming days, around 50,000 employees and more than 1,000 stores in eleven countries (Dr. Web, 4 Nov 2026; GamesWirtschaft, 3 Nov 2026)

  • New: Approval under the Foreign Subsidies Regulation was the last one outstanding. Transactions between JD.com and Ceconomy must run on market terms for ten years.
  • Commitments: According to the December offer, Ceconomy is to stay independent in Europe with its own IT; no changes to staff, employee agreements or locations are planned. Ceconomy cites binding commitments to protect employees, brands and stores, without details.
  • Conditions: Germany cleared the deal in June with conditions on customer data and federal control rights; Austria in October, with conditions that also cover staff in sensitive areas.
  • Relevance: Independence plus a Chinese majority owner is the typical situation in which cooperation between two organisations without a shared culture has to be designed. Amy's cultural shield targets mid-sized companies; this case is a large group and therefore a reference, not a client.
  • Action option: Use the case in conversations with M&A advisers and mid-sized companies: commitments on staff and sites are easy to give, day-to-day alignment with headquarters is the real work.
  • Strategic question: no
  • Sources: Dr. Web: EU gibt Ceconomy-Übernahme durch JD.com frei, Vollzug in den nächsten Tagen (04.11.2026) and GamesWirtschaft: EU genehmigt Übernahme von MediaMarkt-Saturn (03.11.2026)
