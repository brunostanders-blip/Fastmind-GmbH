Market signals

Robotics

1. Rare earths: China extends the suspension of its expanded export controls until 10.01.2027

  • New: China's Ministry of Commerce extended the suspension of the October 2025 measures until 10.01.2027 on 09.11.2026 (S&P Global).
  • Timing: The notice came one day before the old deadline expired (The Star, 09.11.2026).
  • Scope: Controls on five rare earths, processing technology and certain lithium batteries, graphite and products made abroad with Chinese rare earths remain suspended (Benchmark).
  • What stays: The April 2025 licence requirement for seven heavy rare earths, including dysprosium and terbium, remains in force (Benchmark).
  • Relevance for Hanna (robotics): Robot servo motors and joint drives need permanent magnets; the deadline only moves by two months, and the licence requirement for dysprosium and terbium stays.
  • Option: Approach DACH integrators and dealers of Chinese robots on the 10.01.2027 deadline and the supply chain of their drives.
  • Strategic question: yes.

Beite wastewater technology

1. Troisdorf-Müllekoven wastewater plant (Abwasserbetrieb Troisdorf): new sludge digestion plant, agitators described as outdated

  • New: Abwasserbetrieb Troisdorf is building a new sludge digestion plant at the Müllekoven plant (84,000 population equivalent) and describes the existing agitators as outdated (TED 483027-2026).
  • Change: A change notice was published on 30.07.2026 (TED 528247-2026).
  • Status: In 2026 the planning is awarded first, including technical equipment and structural design, with deadlines between August and October 2026 (Bauportal, lot 03).
  • Gaps: Tender for the mechanical equipment, agitator type and schedule not found.
  • Relevance for Hanna (Beite): Whoever plans the technical equipment sets the agitator requirements before construction is tendered.
  • Option: Approach the appointed planning office with a Beite data sheet and CE documents for agitators in sludge and digestion tanks.
  • Strategic question: yes.
