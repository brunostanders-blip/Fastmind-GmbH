Robotics from China, opportunities in DACH: 23 November 2026

Signals for Jan from the Chinese robotics and humanoid market.

China signals

1. Beijing invites foreign robotics firms: embodied AI action plan 2026 to 2028 (relevance: high)
  • What is new: the Beijing Municipal Bureau of Economy and Information Technology published the action plan for embodied AI robots 2026 to 2028 on 11 Nov 2026 (京经信发〔2026〕54号), valid until 31 Dec 2028.
  • Targets for 2028: at least 5 internationally leading and 10 nationally leading humanoid makers, more than 60 % local supply of key parts in the Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei region and 25 % of China's output there.
  • Volume: Beijing plans production capacity of 200,000 robots per year.
  • Invitation abroad: item 17 supports Beijing makers going abroad and encourages foreign robotics firms to set up research centres and manufacturing in Beijing.
  • Bottleneck parts: the plan names tactile sensors, micro precision reducers and coreless motors as parts with supply risk; the target for hands is 0.1 N force resolution at the fingertip and less than 10,000 yuan per hand.
  • Standards: Beijing bodies shall co-write at least 8 international standards; a testing and certification centre for embodied AI robots is planned.
  • Source: „北京市经济和信息化局关于印发《北京市具身智能机器人产业高质量发展行动方案（2026-2028年）》的通知“ (Notice of the Beijing Bureau of Economy and Information Technology on the action plan for high-quality development of the embodied AI robot industry 2026 to 2028), 11 Nov 2026, beijing.gov.cn. Type: primary source, authority.
  • Conformity in the source: no, nothing on CE, the Machinery Regulation or EU standards.
  • Notified body and certificate number named: no.
  • Supply: DACH makers of sensors, reducers and small motors meet a stated demand in Beijing.
  • Approval: more humanoids from Beijing push into export; in Europe the Machinery Regulation applies from 20 Jan 2027 (EUR-Lex).
  • Risk: a site in Beijing ties technology to a programme with 60 % local supply; clarify patents and protection of know-how first.
  • Switzerland: machines with CE marking are recognised in Switzerland.
  • Opportunity for Fastmind: connect DACH suppliers of sensors, reducers and motors with Beijing makers and guide the site path under item 17.
  • Contributes to the strategic question: yes.
2. Hong Kong brings surgical robotics to MEDICA and signs with Medical Valley EMN (relevance: high)
  • What is new: Hong Kong Science and Technology Parks (HKSTP) took part in MEDICA in Düsseldorf for the first time, 16 to 19 Nov 2026, with 22 companies and universities.
  • Robotics: the delegation includes Agilis Robotics, Hopebotics and Yuanhua Robotics, Perception & AI Technologies (HK); the source does not describe the three firms further.
  • Agreements: HKSTP signed memoranda of understanding with Medical Valley EMN (market access, business matching, clinical and research projects) and with Kalms Consulting (regulatory readiness for entering Europe).
  • Visits: Siemens Healthineers, Würth, TUM Venture Labs and SGS Germany; talks on regulation, commercialisation and two-way market access.
  • Source: „HKSTP Debuts at MEDICA 2026, Forging Hong Kong-Europe Pathways for Life and Health Tech“, 19 Nov 2026, hkstp.org. Type: primary source, organiser of the delegation; English release.
  • Conformity in the source: no for the robotics firms; the source names CE only for two diagnostic devices.
  • Notified body and certificate number named: no.
  • Approval: surgical robots need a notified body under the EU MDR; the robotics firms of the delegation have published nothing on this.
  • Sales: no DACH sales partner is named; Medical Valley EMN in Erlangen opens the way to hospitals and research.
  • Switzerland: for medical devices the maker needs a Swiss authorised representative and registration in swissdamed; Swissmedic supervises the market.
  • Opportunity for Fastmind: offer the three robotics firms the path through the EU MDR and to DACH hospitals, alongside the advisory offer of Kalms Consulting.
  • Contributes to the strategic question: yes.
Already known
  • MIIT, five humanoid standards: known since 18.11.
  • New on this: the standard numbers are SJ/T 12353 to SJ/T 12357, including integrated joints (SJ/T 12356) and robot hands (SJ/T 12357), valid from publication (Sina, China.com).
  • Pudu Robotics: known since 29.10.
  • New on this: on 4 Nov 2026 Pudu held its first European partner summit in Amsterdam, with all six product lines and the strategy „One Brain, Multiple Embodiments“ (Robotics & Automation News).

Consequence for DACH

  • Supply: Beijing names the parts it cannot yet supply securely itself: tactile sensors, micro precision reducers, coreless motors (beijing.gov.cn).
  • Site: Beijing explicitly courts research and manufacturing of foreign robotics firms (beijing.gov.cn).
  • Approval: surgical robotics from Hong Kong seeks its way into the EU via Düsseldorf and Erlangen and takes regulatory advice for it (hkstp.org).
  • Standards: China's standards for joints and hands do not replace CE conformity; the Machinery Regulation applies from 20 Jan 2027 (EUR-Lex).
  • Demand in DACH: new tenders for surgical robots or humanoids: sources checked, no new find today.

Content angle

Thesis: China builds the humanoids, but Beijing writes down itself which parts are missing: sensors, reducers, small motors; that is the opportunity for DACH's Mittelstand.
Evidence: the Beijing action plan of 11 Nov 2026 names these bottleneck parts, aims for 60 % local supply by 2028 and invites foreign firms for research and manufacturing (beijing.gov.cn).
