Education and Culture Signals

1. Delegation from Hefei learns about training at the Osnabrück Chamber of Crafts (HWK Osnabrück, 20 Nov 2026)

  • New: A group of 26 people from the partner city of Hefei visited the chamber's training workshops on an education trip.
  • Invitation: The trip was organised by the China Centre (Hochschulzentrum China) of Osnabrück University of Applied Sciences.
  • Interest: The guests asked about the level of training, inter-company training and the role of volunteering in the crafts.
  • Relevance: Osnabrück and Hefei are expanding the corridor, first with the university's International College and now with vocational training. Anyone bringing skilled workers from Hefei to Lower Saxony needs support with arrival.
  • Action: Offer the China Centre a module on orientation and daily life for the Chinese students and trainees who come to Osnabrück through this corridor.
  • Strategic question: yes

2. Eckert Schulen and Shaanxi Technical College of Finance & Economics plan a cooperation platform (Eckert Schulen, 23 Oct 2026)

  • New: The Chinese partner college visited the Regenstauf campus for the second time and handed over a plaque "Sino-German Qinling Workshop" to mark a planned German-Chinese platform.
  • Background: At the first visit in 2024 both sides signed a memorandum of understanding.
  • Plan: Joint projects in vocational training, technology transfer and exchange for students, teachers and skilled workers; the report gives no dates or scope.
  • Relevance: A private education provider is expanding exchange in both directions, including for teachers. This matches Amy's lever of training and integration.
  • Action: Offer Eckert Schulen support for the guests from Shaanxi in Bavaria, from arrival to internship.
  • Strategic question: yes

3. Berlin private school signs a cooperation agreement with a Beijing foreign languages school (Pankower Früchtchen, 4 Nov 2026)

  • New: After a visit by Beijing Xicheng Foreign Languages School to Berlin on 12 Oct 2026, SchuleEins signed a cooperation agreement with the Beijing school; the embassy was represented at the visit.
  • Exchange: A school delegation travelled to Guangxi province from 14 to 23 Oct 2026; last year seven students took part in the exchange.
  • Relevance: An independent school with its own operator builds its China exchange on its own. Such schools decide faster than state schools and are more likely to buy in services.
  • Action: Offer the school preparation and support for the return visit, with host families and intercultural training for the teachers.
  • Strategic question: no

Known since 02.10.: Since 15 Sep 2026 China requires registration of agencies for stays abroad; agencies already active have 90 days, so until mid-December 2026 (Guangming Daily).

Known since 02.11.: The International College of Osnabrück University of Applied Sciences in Hefei opened in April 2026; after the build-up more than 1,000 Chinese students are to study there (Hochschule Osnabrück).

Client Outreach Ideas

  1. University China centres: Institutions like the China Centre in Osnabrück organise delegations and recruit students. Amy offers arrival as a fixed service: pick-up, paperwork with authorities, housing and contact with parents.
  2. Private education providers: Eckert Schulen and similar providers build platforms with Chinese colleges. Amy offers a bilingual package for guests from China, with accommodation, internship companies and intercultural training.
  3. Independent schools with a China exchange: Schools like SchuleEins in Berlin get an offer for the return visit, with host families, programme and support for the young people.

Strategic Question

The sources show that Chinese partners seek cooperation with trainers and technical schools that includes teachers and skilled workers (Eckert Schulen). This calls for profiles that can explain the dual system and organise exchange in vocational training (HWK Osnabrück). Which profiles companies are looking for is not answered by today's sources.

Recommendations for Jan

  • Recommendation: approach the China Centre of Osnabrück University of Applied Sciences as the first partner for an arrival offer, because Osnabrück is expanding both study and vocational training with Hefei and plans for more than 1,000 students.
  • Recommendation: offer Eckert Schulen support for the guests from Shaanxi while the platform is still being planned, because dates and scope are still open.
  • Recommendation: check Amy's placement of Chinese students against the new registration requirement with the Chinese partners before mid-December, because the 90-day transition period from 15 Sep 2026 ends soon.
