6 SWOT for Fastmind

Strengths

  • Access to China and DACH at the same time, based in Düsseldorf.
  • Trades, brokers and advises without manufacturing, so neutral towards makers.
  • Robotics, automation and education as own fields.

Weaknesses

  • Small team, unknown among Bulgarian SMEs.
  • No presence in Bulgaria and no Bulgarian language (assumption).
  • No own expertise for NMPA, CCC, MDR or SORA; needs partners.

Opportunities

  • Funding covers the fair, not the work after it.
  • 10,404 Bulgarian firms already import from China; castings got cheaper in 2025.
  • EU rules favour EU suppliers in DACH over Chinese competitors.

Threats

  • Low willingness to pay and free state competitors.
  • AHK Bulgaria brings German buyers to Bulgarian suppliers for EUR 125 to 750 with federal funding; a competitor for sales into DACH.
  • Only the Bull case beats the median consultant rate of EUR 120 per hour.
  • EU and China tensions, export controls, data law.
