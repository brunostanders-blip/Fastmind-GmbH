## 2. Partner A Research Signals (Industrie & Tech-Compliance)

### Signal 1: DACH Cobot Demand Collapse — The Market Access Paradox
**What's New:**  
Interact Analysis data reveals: while global cobot shipments grow +17% annually (58,000 units 2025 → 120,000 by 2030), Germany shows **−0.3% in 2026** with only 3.7% annual growth through 2030. Meanwhile, India grows 12.5% and Asia-Pacific 7.1%. China's share of global cobots: 50% in 2025, projected two-thirds by 2030.

**Why Relevant for Hanna:**  
Dual-Gateway logic assumes the bottleneck is *market access*—China can't reach DACH, but demand waits. The data says otherwise: the DACH market is shrinking, not closed. Hanna's pitch must shift from "we open the DACH market" to "we segment which parts of DACH actually invest."

**Confidence:** High  
**Recommended Follow-up:** IFR World Robotics Report 2026 (due 24.09.2026) for primary data validation.

**Source:** K-Zeitung (29.06.2026, Interact Analysis data); IFR Pressemitteilung.

### Signal 2: Compliance as Competitive Weapon — European Manufacturers' Defense
**What's New:**  
Franka Robotics CEO statement (INDUSTRIEMAGAZIN, 31.08.2026): "As a German manufacturer, we practically fulfill all standards and are TÜV-certified. Not all Chinese manufacturers are—and therefore cannot sell in Europe." European response strategy: certification/regulatory + collaborative (not humanoid) robotics + compliance under Machinery Regulation (effective 20.01.2027) and AI Act (humanoids = high-risk).

**Counter-Evidence from China:**  
AgiBot (Counterpoint's #1 humanoid maker, 5,000 units shipped by end-2025) enters Europe via Munich, but CEO William Shi emphasizes "long-term partnerships" (no fixed structures); compliance statement only cites GDPR, not CE or Machinery Regulation.

**Why Relevant for Hanna:**  
On the DACH side, compliance is already established as a competitive argument—Hanna isn't the first to mention it. On the China side, a market leader with significant volume is silent on the central market-access question (CE/Machinery Regulation/Notified Body). This gap—communication or preparation—is the negotiation opening.

**Confidence:** High  
**Recommended Follow-up:** Verify whether AgiBot's Munich team has identified a Notified Body for Machinery Regulation compliance.

**Source:** INDUSTRIEMAGAZIN (31.08.2026); ingenieur.de (27.02.2026).

### Signal 3: Textile Compliance Fuse — 14 Days to First Hard Deadline
**What's New:**  
**Empowering Consumers Directive (EU) 2024/825 takes effect 27.09.2026** (in 14 days from run date). Starting that date, banned without transition: generic environmental claims ("eco-friendly," "green," "biodegradable"), uncertified sustainability labels, and carbon-neutral claims resting on external compensation. **No transition period; includes existing inventory.**

Regulatory timeline consolidation for Hanna's two fields:

| Date | Regulation | Scope | Field |
|---|---|---|---|
| **27.09.2026** | **EmpCo (EU) 2024/825** | **Generic green claims banned (B2C, indirect B2B via supply chain)** | **Textiles** |
| 20.01.2027 | Machinery Regulation 2023/1230 | Full applicability; MRL 2006/42 repealed | Robotics |
| 02.12.2027 | AI Act | High-risk obligations (Annex III) | Robotics |
| 02.08.2028 | AI Act (Digital Omnibus) | High-risk in regulated products (Annex I) | Robotics |

**Why Relevant for Hanna:**  
In both fields, the *first binding* deadline is NOT the headline (AI Act 2027, DPP 2028), but an earlier one (Machinery Regulation 20.01.2027 for robotics; EmpCo 27.09.2026 for textiles). The Textiles deadline is 14 days away.

**Confidence:** High (regulatory source: EUR-Lex, primary)  
**Recommended Follow-up:** Identify textile/fiber suppliers with sustainability claims in current collateral; 2-week outreach window.

**Source:** Richtlinie (EU) 2024/825 (EUR-Lex); coslaw.eu; ventum-consulting.
